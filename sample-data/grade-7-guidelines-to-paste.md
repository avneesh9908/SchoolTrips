## Safety guidelines

```
A ratio of 1:12 is maintained (one accompanying adult for every 10 students), including teachers, admin, support staff and Safety Monitors.
Room allocation lets teachers easily monitor students; students know which room their teacher is in.
One adult accompanies students at all times, including washroom breaks and activities.
A student orientation is held 15 days before the trip covering safety protocols.
A trained medical person and first-aid facility are available at the campsite at all times.
Nearest Police Station and Hospital details are kept handy with the Safety Monitor.
Accompanying staff receive first-aid training and carry contact details of all parents.
Staff and vendors are trained under POCSO, with signed undertakings from all vendors.
Each batch has 2 dedicated Safety Monitors focused solely on student safety.
Regular WhatsApp updates with photos/videos are shared with parents.
Headcount is taken at every stop and at regular intervals; health checks are done daily at end of day.
```

## Do/Dont's

Keep the `Do:` and `Don't:` prefixes — the app uses them to split the one column into the
two-column Do / Don't layout. Lines without a prefix are printed as a single list.

```
Do: If your child takes any medicine regularly, hand it to the Safety Monitor a day prior at the bus stop, with clear instructions.
Do: Inform the accompanying teacher if your child has any medical history needing personal attention.
Don't: Students may not carry personal medicines — staff carry a first-aid box with basics if needed.
Don't: Students may not carry mobile phones, smart watches, or other gadgets; these will be confiscated if found.
```

## Things to carry

```
Original School ID & Aadhar card (mandatory)
Clothes for 7 days — full pants, full-sleeve T-shirts, sweatshirts
Sweater / fleece jacket / thermals
Night dress
Heavy woolens
Shawl / light blanket
Sport or trekking shoes (compulsory)
Sandals / slippers
Water bottle + shoulder bag to carry it throughout the day
Towel, napkin, cap/hat, bandana, cotton socks, warm gloves
Toiletries, moisturizer, sun cream, lip balm
Mosquito repellent cream — Odomos (compulsory)
Dry, healthy snacks for travelling
```

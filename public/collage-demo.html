Trip photos

Move your cursor across the pictures, then scroll down. Four things happen:

  1. Lean — a tile tips toward the pointer, so it turns as you move across it.
  2. Light — a highlight follows the cursor inside the tile, like glass catching the light.
  3. Lift — hover pulls the tile forward out of the page, and the photograph inside drifts a little further than its frame.
  4. Arrive — tiles rise into place as you scroll down to them, a few at a time, instead of all of them before you get there. The big picture also drifts slowly on its own, so the page is never completely still.

These are placeholder stock images, not the school's photographs — the trip-photo folders in Drive are shared with named people rather than "anyone with the link", so nothing can be read from them yet. The layout and the motion are exactly what the real Photos tab does.

Your system has "reduce motion" turned on, so the animation is switched off — this is what that reader sees.
